# iHealth — Medication Management and Reminders App

## Project Overview

iHealth is a **conceptual mobile health application** designed to help older adults in Malaysia manage their daily health routines more easily and independently. It brings together medication reminders, access to healthcare, caregiver support, health monitoring, and community features in one proposed platform.

The project focuses on **accessibility and ease of use**, with interface decisions intended to make common tasks less confusing for elderly users. A high-fidelity prototype was designed in Figma to present the app concept and demonstrate how its key features could work.

## Problem and Objectives

Keeping track of medication schedules and health information can be challenging for older adults. Some may also need extra support from family members or caregivers and more convenient ways to access healthcare services.

iHealth aims to:

- Make medication schedules and reminders easier to manage.
- Design an interface that is practical for elderly users.
- Support communication and shared health monitoring with family members or caregivers.
- Explore ways to connect healthcare services, wellness tracking, and community engagement.

## Proposed Features

| Feature | How it is intended to help |
| --- | --- |
| **Medication management and reminders** | Organize medication schedules and provide customizable, voice-supported reminders. |
| **Telemedicine** | Offer a way for users to access remote medical consultations. |
| **Family and caregiver support** | Enable shared visibility of relevant health and medication information. |
| **Health tracking dashboard** | Present health information, with a proposed connection to data from wearable devices. |
| **Gamification and community forum** | Encourage positive routines and social interaction to help reduce isolation. |

## UI/UX Design Approach

The prototype was designed with the needs of elderly users in mind. Key design considerations included:

- **Large, recognizable icons** to make important actions easier to identify.
- **Simple screen layouts** to reduce unnecessary complexity.
- **Voice-command and voice-reminder concepts** to support more accessible interactions.
- **Clear feature organization** so users can locate medication, monitoring, and support functions more easily.

**Design tool:** Figma  
**Prototype:** [View iHealth Prototype on Figma](https://www.figma.com/file/U4S0Xc4Xgyywh2YKE9wDwl/iHealth-Prototype)

## Project Workflow

1. **Problem identification:** Explored the challenges older adults may face with medication routines and healthcare access.
2. **System analysis:** Identified the target users, proposed features, and overall application needs.
3. **Interface design:** Planned user-friendly screens and interactions with accessibility in mind.
4. **High-fidelity prototyping:** Created the iHealth interface and connected the proposed user experience in Figma.
5. **Concept presentation:** Presented the app's purpose, feature ideas, and design approach.
